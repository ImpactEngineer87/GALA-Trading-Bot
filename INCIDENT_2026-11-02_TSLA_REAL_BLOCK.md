## November 3 recovery

The account owner reconnected Schwab through the dashboard. A fresh broker check at 13:07:51 UTC found zero positions and zero pending orders. The client agreed to pause GALA before any further manual trade in a monitored REAL ticker. During stopped-service maintenance, `live_trading.reviewed_manual_tsla_resume` was enabled and a root-owned grant for the 2026-11-03 New York session was issued after the issuer repeated the broker, guard, archive, history and risk checks. The five accounting records and their unknown historical P/L were not changed.

At 13:09 UTC (08:09 NY), the engine started through the normal dashboard control in MIXED mode. Live preflight approved BA, META, NVDA, TSLA and NKE as REAL. A subsequent dashboard check reported `THREAD_RUNNING`, Schwab connected, `entry_blocked=false`, `manual_tsla_resume_active=true`, five unresolved records, daily risk unblocked, and broker reconciliation flat. This proves the accounting gate is open for the current session; it does not prove that a later signal will satisfy every trading and fill condition. The grant expires at the next New York session and any new discrepancy blocks entries immediately. A fresh review is required for a later session while historical records remain unresolved.

## November 3 manual-trade upgrade (v3.3.16)

A GALA-managed REAL position closed from a thinkorswim chart can now be recognized from a single exact `API_TOS:CHART` exit fill, provided Schwab confirms the symbol flat with no working order, the audited GALA entry matches the broker's entry fill, the exit quantity and price come from complete execution legs, and no other external fill followed that entry. The exit's gross P/L counts toward the REAL daily loss guard. The close is marked manual and excluded from GALA performance, model feedback and shadow labels. Missing or ambiguous evidence still creates the existing REAL accounting block.

An untracked position in a monitored REAL ticker is no longer automatically adopted merely because it exists at Schwab. Startup, periodic orphan recovery, order cleanup and the shutdown sweep require a GALA-submitted order ID and matching broker fills before changing a position or working order. Unknown/manual exposure quarantines REAL entries while it exists; two consecutive broker reads proving the symbol flat and without working orders clear that transient quarantine. The client agreed to pause GALA before future manual trades in monitored REAL tickers.

The five older accounting records remain unresolved and their historical P/L remains unknown. `live_trading.reviewed_manual_tsla_future_sessions` is a separate opt-in: with the exact reviewed guard/grant unchanged, a future NY session may use the reviewed exception only after a fresh startup Schwab check proves the account flat with no pending orders. A new accounting discrepancy invalidates the exact guard hash and blocks entries. The PAPER loss limit remains $700 and the REAL loss limit remains $200.
